# Support

- **Questions:** ask in
  [GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/mahiti-org/convene/discussions).
- **Bugs:** open an [issue](https://github.com/mahiti-org/convene/issues) with
  steps to reproduce. Do not include real beneficiary data.
- **Security issues:** see [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
- **NGOs needing help** with deployment, hosting or training: email
  convene@mahiti.org.
