# Roadmap

Near-term priorities. These may change based on the needs of NGOs using
Convene.

- **Duplicate detection:** fuzzy matching of beneficiaries and a merge workflow
  for potential duplicates.
- **Groups and federations:** groups module with membership, savings and bank
  linkage.
- **Reporting and web admin:** reports and dashboards from form responses, and
  a complete web admin.

## Proposing changes

Suggest ideas in
[GitHub Discussions](https://github.com/mahiti-org/convene/discussions). To
work on a roadmap item, comment on the related issue first.
