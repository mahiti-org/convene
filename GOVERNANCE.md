# Governance

## Steward

Convene is stewarded by [Mahiti](https://mahiti.org). Mahiti owns the
repository, appoints maintainers, decides on releases and has the final say
when maintainers cannot agree.

## Maintainers

Maintainers are the Convene core team at Mahiti. They review and merge pull
requests, triage issues and keep the roadmap up to date.

## Decision making

Maintainers make decisions by consensus in issues, discussions and pull
requests. If they cannot agree, Mahiti decides.

## Becoming a maintainer

Contributors who make regular, high-quality contributions may be invited by the
maintainers to join them. Mahiti confirms the invitation.
