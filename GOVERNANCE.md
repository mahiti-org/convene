# Governance

Convene is a open-source project. This document explains how it will grow with the community.

## Who runs Convene today

[Mahiti](https://mahiti.org) started Convene and looks after it. Mahiti appoints maintainers, plans releases and makes the final call when people cannot agree.

We want Convene to be shaped by the NGOs and developers who use it. As more people contribute, we will share these decisions more widely (see [growing with the community](#growing-with-the-community)).

## Roles

- **Contributors** are anyone who reports a bug, answers a question, translates, writes docs, tests, or sends code. No permission is needed.
- **Maintainers** review and merge pull requests, triage issues and plan releases. Contributors who help regularly and well are invited to become maintainers. Maintainers can come from any organisation.

## How decisions are made

- **Everyday changes** happen in issues and pull requests. A pull request needs one maintainer's approval and passing checks.
- **Bigger changes**, such as new modules, changes to the data model or breaking changes, start as a GitHub Discussion so users and contributors can comment before work begins.
- **Decisions are made in public.** If something is discussed in a call or a Mahiti meeting, a short summary is posted in Discussions.
- **If maintainers cannot agree,** Mahiti decides and explains why.

## Code of conduct

Everyone follows our [code of conduct](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md). Report a concern through the [contact form](https://convene.mahiti.org/#hello) by choosing "Report a conduct concern". At least two maintainers handle each report confidentially. Anyone named in a report takes no part in handling it.

## Growing with the community

We will review this document once a year in public, or sooner when the community grows. Once there are regular maintainers from outside Mahiti, we will share release planning and the final say on disagreements with them. Later, if several organisations depend on Convene, we may move it to a neutral home such as a foundation.
